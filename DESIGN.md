Following our idea of theming the project as an old-time movie theater, these are the design specifications we have set.

1. Landing page
   1. Make the floor wood, the same wood as the stage
   2. The user still faces the same direction, and the room is unchanged, but the contents of the room have changed. Instead, you have a concessions stand.
   3. The concessions stand consists of multiple menu pages displayed across the top, a wooden counter, and a popcorn machine popping quietly to the right behind the counter. The back wall (left side) has a framed poster which shows a monochrome, looped snapshot of Projector characters moving from the finish line/appearing back at the beginning. “PROJECTOR” is written across the top. Nothing else.
   4. Instructions will be displayed in posters across the top of the booth, replacing menus options in traditional popcorn booths. This makes for a total of four posters, in a row. 
   5. Popcorn will be popping in the right
2. Projector room
   1. The room should be expanded by many times, length and width, to accommodate dark red seats that fade into black (as opposed to hard walls) which face the projector
   2. The ceiling should be 3x as high
   3. Dim lighting
   4. The stage, with its wooden floor, will hold the table, projector screen, and an old-school projector on a black stand.
      1. The projector screen has a curtain on either side, tied with dark gold rope
      2. Extend the projector by 3x wide, 2x tall
      3. The boundaries of the stage are the boundaries of player movement
   5. Start 2D game menu
      1. Instead of a rectangular window, use a thick tasseled rope hanging down from the ceiling, located at the same place the original menu was
      2. The wall has a square of light cast onto it, with fuzzy edges. On it, in black text, are instructions.
      3. When the game has not started, instruct “Pull to start”
      4. When the game is in progress, instruct the other option that currently exists.
   6. Projector screen design
      1. Make the background off-white, with noise, as if there is some film noise at hand
      2. Dull the colors onscreen
